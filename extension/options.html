Jev Chat for Twitch

Your key is stored in this browser profile only, sent only to api.typesafe.ai, and never logged.

Stored in chrome.storage.local. Clear the field and save to remove it. Estimated at $0.042 per million input tokens. When the cap is reached the column stops calling Jev and says so. Messages above this rate are counted as unassessed. They are never shown as selected.

Back to Twitch: open any live channel at https://www.twitch.tv/<channel> and the Jev Chat column appears on the right. The gear in that column brings you back here.

Message text and usernames from the channel you are watching are sent to TypeSafe under your own account. Nothing is persisted except these settings.
